The kitchen · the bar

Pub grub, elevated

The plan, in What Now Boston’s words: “a refined take on pub fare, with elevated dishes drawing inspiration from both Irish and New England traditions” — food that keeps pub plainness out of its vocabulary but keeps the pub’s welcome intact.

Two traditions on one plate

Irish · New England

Irish pub cooking and New England cooking were always cousins: butter, shellfish, soda bread, stews, the Sunday roast that feeds six people in a small kitchen. The kitchen here is being planned as the elevated version of that shared table — pub grub done with technique, not pub grub done away. The same hands run the group’s other rooms: Broadside Tavern on the waterfront, The Merchant Kitchen & Drinks.

The board turns with the season

Whiskey · cocktails · wine · beer

The published plan: “an extensive, seasonally shifting beverage program… whiskey, cocktails, wine, and thoughtfully chosen beers.” For an Irish pub, the whiskey shelf is the headline; the founding team’s own statement puts “a great pint” in the same breath as great music and a beautiful room.
