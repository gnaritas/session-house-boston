The people · the room

Coming home to Adams Street

“I’m from Ireland, but I say I grew up in this area because my first apartment was on Adams Street.” Frank Delaney told the licensing board that, in September 2026, asking them to approve the pub he’s putting in the old Scobie’s space — his children went to St. Brendan’s, he said, and his grandchildren go there now.

Esteemed Hospitality, LLC

The group behind the pub

Delaney runs Broadside Tavern and The Merchant Kitchen & Drinks in Boston, and formerly ran The Times Irish Pub & Restaurant. Session House is the group’s newest concept, with Teresa Murphy as managing partner — the voice on the licensing filings and the one guiding the renovation.

The building

The old Scobie’s space, given a second life

782 Adams Street has been a pub corner of the neighborhood’s memory since the family-run Scobie’s opened there in September 2024. Scobie’s closed in February 2026, and Session House is taking the space with light renovations: rich dark tones, warm gold accents, traditional musical imagery — built, in What Now Boston’s report, to feel like a well-established gathering place rather than a new restaurant. The interior is being re-designed with artist Vincent Crotty.
